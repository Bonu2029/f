How to find your Google Review link

  1. Sign in to the Google account that manages your business.
  2. Search for your business name on Google.
  3. Open your Google Business Profile.
  4. Select “Read reviews.”
  5. Select “Get more reviews.”
  6. Select “Copy link.”
  7. Return to this page and paste the link into the Google Review Link field.
