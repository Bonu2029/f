# AFTER THE FLASH · Episode 5 — "HIDE"

Canon: Episode 4 ended on a poll. The audience voted **A · HIDE IN THE BOOTH**. Nobody shines the light.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs — use the SAME voice as Ep1–3: "Adam - Brooding Dark Tough"; one paragraph per line, short pauses between lines)

```
Most of you said hide.
Fifteen people. One ticket booth. It fits six.
The others lie flat behind the benches.
Rosa reaches over and turns off your phone light.
Total dark.
The hum becomes a rumble.
Wind pushes out of the tunnel. Dust in your eyes.
Then a light. Slow. Swinging.
It's not a train.
It's a cart on the rails. Pushed by people in white suits and masks.
They stop at the platform.
One of them climbs up. A flashlight in one hand.
Rosa leans close. "That's not our uniform," she whispers.
The light moves along the wall. Bench to bench.
A muffled voice behind the mask: "Fifteen."
Then: "There should be sixteen."
Biscuit starts to growl. You hold his mouth shut.
Rule five: In the dark, stay quiet.
Light and noise tell them where you are.
The figure kneels at the lost and found box.
He takes the yellow raincoat. Folds it under his arm.
He stands up. On his back, a big black letter.
T.
Then the radio on the cart crackles.
A child's voice: "Did you find them?"
The figure turns. And looks straight at the booth.
So… what would you do?
Step out and talk to him…
or stay hidden?
```
