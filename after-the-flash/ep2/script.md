# AFTER THE FLASH · Episode 2 — "KEEP IT SHUT"

Canon: Episode 1 ended on a poll. The audience voted **B · KEEP IT SHUT**. The gate stays locked.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs — same voice as Ep1, one paragraph per line, short pauses between lines)

```
Most of you said keep it shut.
So Rosa keeps the key in her fist. The gate stays locked.
The man stops banging. He sits down on the stairs.
"Please," he says. Quieter this time.
Fifteen people. One bottle of water. Nobody looks at the gate.
Except Biscuit.
He won't stop watching the little girl.
She hasn't moved. She hasn't blinked. Not once.
Hour four. The ash outside is getting thicker.
Rosa sits down next to you.
"Twenty-two years on this line," she says. "I've never locked this gate."
Rule two: Stay inside for at least twenty-four hours.
The first day is the most dangerous.
Your phone. Thirty-eight percent. No signal.
Hour five. The coughing stops.
You look up the stairs. The man is asleep against the bars.
The little girl is gone.
Her stuffed rabbit is lying on the step. Inside the gate.
Nobody opened it. Rosa still has the key.
Biscuit sniffs the rabbit… and backs away.
Then, from the dark tunnel behind you… a child laughs.
Every light on the platform goes out. Except one.
So… what would you do?
Follow the laugh into the tunnel…
or wake the man at the gate?
```
