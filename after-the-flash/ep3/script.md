# AFTER THE FLASH · Episode 3 — "WAKE THE MAN"

Canon: Episode 2 ended on a poll. The audience voted **B · WAKE THE MAN**. Nobody follows the laugh (yet).

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs — same Adam voice as Ep1/Ep2, one paragraph per line, short pauses between lines)

```
Most of you said wake the man.
You turn on your phone light. Thirty-five percent.
You walk up the stairs to the gate.
"Hey. Hey! Wake up."
You shake the bars. He doesn't move.
Then his eyes open.
He looks at the empty step beside him.
"Where is she?"
Rosa holds up the stuffed rabbit.
"It was inside the gate," she says.
The man goes pale.
"She's not my daughter."
"I found her on the street. She grabbed my hand."
"She said the gate would open for her."
Nobody says a word.
Rule three: If you were outside, take off your outer clothes.
Brush off the ash. It removes most of the danger.
Rosa pushes a trash bag through the bars.
The man drops his jacket into it.
Something falls out of the pocket.
A child's drawing. The station. The tunnel.
And one letter, circled in red.
Biscuit turns toward the tunnel. And growls.
The last red light flickers.
Then a small voice, from the dark:
"Biscuit… come here, boy."
She knows his name.
So… what would you do?
Open the gate and let the man in…
or go after the voice?
```
